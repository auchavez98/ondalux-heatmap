This policy explains what data is processed when you visit ondaluxheatmap.com (the "website") and when you use the Ondalux Heatmap desktop application (the "app").

The app

The app runs entirely on your computer. It does not require an account, it does not send your floor plans, projects or survey data anywhere, and it contains no analytics or telemetry. Your projects are saved only where you choose to save them.

When you survey a site, the app reads the list of nearby WiFi networks from your operating system to build the measured heat map. That information is stored only in your project file.

The website

Server logs

Like any website, our hosting provider records technical information about each request — IP address, browser and operating system, requested page, date and time — to operate and protect the service. These logs are kept for a limited time and are not used to identify you.

Local storage

If you close the language suggestion, the website stores a single preference in your browser so it is not shown again. It contains no personal data and never leaves your device.
